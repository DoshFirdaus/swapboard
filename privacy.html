Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

KakiMart (kakimart.sg and the KakiMart Android app) is a free community marketplace in Singapore where people list items, chat, and meet in person to buy and sell. KakiMart is run by an individual in Singapore. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your choices, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and display name. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our database provider; nobody at KakiMart can see it.
  • Listings: titles, descriptions, prices, categories, meet-up areas and photos you post. These are public: anyone visiting KakiMart can see them.
  • Chats: messages you send to other users through KakiMart.
  • Reports: reports you file about listings, including your reason.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address, device and browser type, and times of access, kept by our service providers for security and to keep the service running.

We do not collect your NRIC, payment details, precise location, or contacts. KakiMart does not process payments; buyers pay sellers directly.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your listings and let buyers and sellers chat.
  • To keep KakiMart safe: investigating reports, scams, prohibited items and disputes, and enforcing our rules.
  • To send essential emails, such as account confirmation.
  • To meet legal obligations, including requests from authorities.

We do not sell your personal data. KakiMart currently shows no ads and does not share your data with advertisers.

3. Who can see your chats

Chats are private between the buyer and seller. However, as the operator, KakiMart can access chat messages. We only review them when a report or dispute is raised, to investigate suspected scams or misuse, or when required by law. We may keep chats and reports after a listing is deleted, so that disputes can still be investigated.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers to run KakiMart. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, photo storage and chat. Data is hosted in Supabase's Singapore region.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the KakiMart website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and website code; they may see your IP address when pages load.
  • Google Play: distributes the Android app.

Some providers may process data outside Singapore. Where that happens, we rely on them to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account is active. Listings stay until you delete them. Chats and reports may be kept for up to 2 years after the last activity to handle disputes and safety issues, unless the law requires longer. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, listings and photos. We keep a private record of your email and display name, together with chats and reports you were part of, for up to 2 years for safety, dispute and legal purposes.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct it.
  • Delete your listings: you can delete any listing yourself in the app.
  • Delete your account: use "Delete my account" on the Me page in the app, or email us from the address linked to your account at kakimart.sg@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Deletion in the app is immediate; by email we will complete it within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop using KakiMart and ask us to delete your data at any time.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, access to other users' data is restricted by database security rules, and passwords are hashed. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and never share it.

8. Age

KakiMart is intended for users aged 18 and above. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Safety reminder

Only share personal details, such as your phone number or address, in chats when you are comfortable. Meet in public places and pay on collection.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we will give notice in the app.

11. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: kakimart.sg@gmail.com
