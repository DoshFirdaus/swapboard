Delete your KakiMart account

KakiMart (kakimart.sg and the KakiMart Android app)

Option 1: in the app or website (immediate)

  1. Log in to KakiMart.
  2. Tap Me at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account and follow the steps to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (within 30 days)

If you can't log in, email kakimart.sg@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request before we delete anything, to make sure it really comes from you.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (display name and account)
  • All your listings and their photos
  • Your phone's notification address

What is kept, and for how long

To handle disputes, scams and safety issues, we keep a private record of your email address and display name, together with chats and reports you were part of, for up to 2 years. This is then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. These records are not shown to other users.

You can also delete individual listings at any time without deleting your account. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Questions: kakimart.sg@gmail.com
